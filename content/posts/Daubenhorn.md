Location: Leukerbad, Valais, Switzerland.Summit Altitude: 2,941 meters (9,649 feet) above sea level.Difficulty: ED (Extremely Difficult), rated K5 to K6.Stats: Over 2,000 meters of steel cable, 216 meters of ladders, and roughly 920 to 1,000 meters of vertical elevation gain.Duration: About 6 to 8 hours total (including access and descent).Season: Open typically from July to October, depending on snow and weather
