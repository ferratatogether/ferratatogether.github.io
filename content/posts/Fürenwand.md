You MUST sign the waiver form, it contains detailed information about the difficulty and more: https://forms.gle/RDFZBHhXgt5dwo2bA

You can read the QuickStart guide:https://docs.google.com/document/d/19oks3FaopmkEDDOAUF163Lo_qWMmBgFS0Ht5TqTf0YA/edit?usp=sharing

There is no transport organised, no Bus.
You can use your car or travel with the organiser Cédric Walter from Zürich HB, meeting up below the Blue Engel at 6:15 sharp
The recommended train will be announced here later

Costs:
You'll have to pay for:
* Transport by train to Engelberg or you organise your own transportation
* Renting the Via Ferrata set equipment or bringing your own
(Difficulty: K4/K5 – difficult) Following 750 m up a steep exposed wall, mostly on metal bars or rods. Very airy throughout the entire route, you should not be afraid of heights on this one.

The hanging ladder at the top is a real deal 🤩

🧗 Gear: Harness, lanyards, helmet, gloves, sturdy shoes water, snacks (+ sticks for descent if you choose to hike or the cable car is closed)

👉 Start: Hike 30min from Engelberg valley station of Fürenalp cable car

⬆️ Climb: 2.5h

⬇️ Descent: From Fürenalp summit → trail to Engelberg (1h) or cable car down
