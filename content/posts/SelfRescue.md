Security and gear discovery, knowing your equipment meet-up.
Will be executed in a flat area or on some stairs backward for safety.

Avoiding accidents
- [ ] Proper optional usage of resting lanyard on bridge, horizontal section, during difficult long and vertical climb with ladders
- [ ] Tips
- [ ] Using properly a Dyneema loop 
- [ ] Correct usage of PETZL zip line pulley 

Bare minimum self rescue

Going back to safety
- [ ] Basic knot Shirley stopping knot, Prussik friction knot, 8 knot, Clove Hitch
- [ ] 8 rappelling down or belaying
- [ ] Rappelling down using a PETZL gri gri+, a PETZL reverso
- [ ] Rappelling vertically UP after a fall with either
    - [ ] 2 Prussik knots + Sling 
    - [ ] Petzl Croll + Petzl Ascension
- [ ] Self Rescue on a bridge

Helping others
- [ ] Rappelling up or assisting a person using PETZL safety kit 
- [ ] Petzl TiBloc and capture pulley
