The Daubenhorn Via Ferrata in Leukerbad is the longest via ferrata in Switzerland.   Over 2,000 meters of steel cable, 216 meters of ladders, and roughly 920 to 1,000 meters of vertical elevation gain.

{{< notice info>}} 👨‍⚖️
Newcomers [MUST sign the waiver form](https://forms.gle/phYLQZ5mvnNeuR9N9), it contains detailed information about the risks difficulty and more
{{< /notice >}}

{{< notice tip>}} 📓 
The [Ferrata Together QuickStart Guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19oks3FaopmkEDDOAUF163Lo_qWMmBgFS0Ht5TqTf0YA) is a good start to learn how to execute via ferrata in safety, a must read for beginners and experienced climbers. It contains a lot of tips.
{{< /notice >}}
   
## Difficulty 📈

ED (Extremely Difficult), rated K5 to K6.
    
## Duration ⏱️

About 6 to 8 hours total (including access and descent).

## Season 🗓️

Typically June through late September (conditions permitting)
