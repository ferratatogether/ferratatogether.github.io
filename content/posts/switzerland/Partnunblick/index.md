## Route overview ℹ️
The Partnunblick via ferrata is a demanding, athletic continuation of the easier Family Via Ferrata located just below it. Set against premium, compact Rätikon limestone, the route weaves straight up the steep **Gemstobelwand**. 

It alternates between sharp vertical slabs, airy exposed traverses, and short overhanging walls that require solid technique and upper-body power (rated K4 to K5 / D/E). The exit lands right on the national border line dividing Switzerland and Austria. Climbers can also extend their day at the summit ridge by making a 15-minute push to the top of the **Sulzfluh (2817m)**.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! Rätikon limestone loses all grip immediately if rain or damp mountain fog sets in.

## Season 🗓️

Typically **late June through mid-October** (depending entirely on when early snow clears from the deep Gemstobel scree corridors).

Board the InterCity train heading east from Zürich HB to Landquart, transfer directly to the regional line towards Küblis, and board Postbus line 214 up into the mountain valleys of St. Antönien.

## Cable cars 🚠
*No major cable car railways exist for this route.* Transport relies entirely on the local valley Postbuses and the optional alpine on-demand shuttle vans.

## By Car 🚗

Take the A14/A13 motorway to Landquart, follow national route 28 through the Prättigau valley until you reach Küblis, and then turn up the steep mountain switchbacks toward St. Antönien and Partnun.

Park explicitly at ** hikers' parking lot P6 (Partnun)**. This is the last public parking zone in the valley; driving past this terminal requires a special permit.

## Water 🚰
Completely absent once you step onto the steep limestone wall. Pack a minimum of 1.5L of water to stay hydrated during the heavy vertical climbing steps.

Get water or drinks at:
- Berghaus Sulzfluh (Partnun valley floor)
- Berghaus Alpenrösli (Partnun valley floor)

## Donation 💶
The via ferratas in St. Antönien are developed and safely maintained by local alpine sports initiatives and mountain rescue teams. Leaving a voluntary contribution in the donation boxes along the valley approaches helps preserve the steel lines.

## My review ⭐️
A stellar addition to the Rätikon climbing scene! Using the Family Via Ferrata as a warm-up is a great way to start the day before jumping into the steep vertical pitches of the main face. The rock quality is fantastic, compact limestone with plenty of friction, and looking straight down onto the turquoise Lake Partnun provides incredible exposure. Be sure to stop at Berghaus Sulzfluh for a fresh slice of nut cake on your way down!
