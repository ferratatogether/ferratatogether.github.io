## Route overview ℹ️
This is the configuration for **La Face (The North Face Route / Voie Hohl)** variant on Le Moléson. It is a highly athletic, strictly **one-way trail with no intermediate exits**. 

Unlike the companion *Pilier* route, *La Face* plunges you directly into the cold, shadow-cast limestone of the sheer North Wall. It follows a highly direct line featuring continuous vertical rungs, slick athletic rock pillars, exposed grassy ledges, and a physical, sustained **overhanging crux sequence** near the upper third that tests your pure upper-body endurance. The route merges with the pillar route just below the summit ridge cross.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! Because the North Face sits entirely in the shade, the rock blocks dry very slowly after rainfall and can remain bitterly cold and slick even during mid-summer.

## Water 🚰
Completely absent inside the sheer, rock chimney faces. Bring a full hydration container before entering the cable networks.

Get water or drinks at:
- Restaurant de Plan-Francey (Funicular mid-station)
- Summit Restaurant (Le Moléson mountain top terminal)

## My review ⭐️
An intense, pure vertical test piece! While the Pilier route gets the warm sunshine, *La Face* is dark, intimidating, and pushes your arm endurance immediately. The exposure looking straight down into the void beneath your boots is wild, and the overhanging crux ladder sections require efficient clip work so your forearms don't blow out. Bring warm layers—even in summer, the wind howling against this shadow wall is cold!
