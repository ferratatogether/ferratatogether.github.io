## Route overview ℹ️
The route is heavily characterized by its "adventure park" style elements built into the natural alpine environment.
• A 30-metre suspension bridge.
• A 40-metre three-cable Nepali bridge.
• A horizontal ladder running directly beneath the cable car line.
• A Tyrolienne (zip line), though this element is bypassed on the normal route and can only be used if you are accompanied by a licensed mountain guide.

The first half of the via ferrata steps down out of the station and traverses horizontally across grippy rock and alpine slopes (the Schneefink-Querung and Gemstritt). This section is highly scenic and relatively easy.
Before the real vertical challenge begins at the Mauerläuferplatte, there is a clearly marked emergency exit (Notausstieg). This makes the route highly accessible; if you or a companion find the heights too daunting after the bridges, you can safely exit back to the trail. The second half brings steep vertical ascents on iron staples up to the Adlersitz ridge before concluding at the mountain peak.
