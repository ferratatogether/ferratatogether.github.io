## Route overview ℹ️
The Via Ferrata du Belvédère is a magnificent, panoramic route built directly onto the prominent rock spur below the village of Nax. It offers a spectacular, vertical bird's-eye view stretching across the Rhone Valley and the city of Sion. The trail features vertical walls, athletic rock corners, and exposed horizontal traverses well-equipped with iron steps and rungs. An intermediate escape route is available mid-way before the final vertical wall push. The climb culminates elegantly at a wooden observation platform right at the top of the cliff face.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! Due to its lower altitude and sunny exposure, the rock can get exceptionally hot in the direct sun.

## Season 🗓️

Accessible **year-round** when snow-free due to its south-facing orientation and low elevation. The formal prime climbing season runs from **April through November**.

## Cable cars 🚠
*No cable cars are required for this route.* The entire cliff face is easily accessible directly on foot from the road and village parking areas.

## By Car 🚗

Drive along the A9 motorway through Valais, exit smoothly at exit 27 (Sion-Est), and follow the signs climbing up the switchbacks toward Bramois and Nax.

## Water 🚰
Completely absent along the sun-baked rock face. Ensure you carry at least 1L of fluids to handle the climb comfortably.

Get water or drinks at:
- Local shops and bakeries in Nax village center (10-minute walk from the exit)

## My review ⭐️
A beautiful route with unmatched valley views! It offers the perfect step up for advanced beginners or intermediates looking for vertical exposure without overly exhausting overhangs. Standing on the vertical face looking down at the winding Rhone River is mesmerizing, and completing the climb directly at the panoramic picnic deck makes for a perfect finish.
