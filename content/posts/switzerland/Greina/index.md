## Route overview ℹ️
This template covers the **secured alpine path segments** of the majestic Greina Plateau traverse. Rather than an artificial sports park, this is a raw, high-alpine mountain trail (graded SAC T2/T3). 

The technical safety highlights are split across two spectacular areas:
### The Sumvitg Suspension Bridge
A spectacular 65-metre long and 40-metre high pedestrian suspension bridge spanning the rushing waters of the *Rein da Sumvitg*.

### The Western Gorge Chains
The steep, exposed rock steps and slippery slabs dropping from the **Greina Pass (2358m)** down into the *Pian Geirett* basin floor. These are fully protected with fixed heavy steel chains to guarantee secure handholds for hikers.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! Due to its wide, exposed, and tree-less high-altitude topography, the plateau turns incredibly hazardous during dense mountain fog or sudden afternoon lightning storms.

## Season 🗓️

Typically open from **early July through late September or mid-October**, strictly depending on when heavy winter snowpacks fully clear the high passes.

Drive past Chur along the A13 motorway, take exit 18 (Reichenau) toward Disentis/Ilanz, and turn into the mountain valleys at Ilanz to drive past Lumbrein straight to the public lots at the edge of Vrin.

From Puzzatsch, follow the marked singletrack climbing steadily across the open alpine pastures of Alp Diesrut up to the **Pass Diesrut (2427m)**. Descend briefly into the vast tundra plain, cross the Sumvitg suspension bridge, and pass the Terrihütte to cross the flat marshlands to the Greina Pass marker.{{< /sac-scale >}}

## Travelling home 🏠

Board the specialized southern *Bus alpin* regional alpine shuttle directly from the Pian Geirett turnaround loop to descend down to Ghirone and Campo Blenio for connections back to the main rail lines.

## Water 🚰
Do not drink untreated river or marsh water directly from the tundra braids due to heavy sheep grazing filtration variables. Pack a robust clean water supply.

Get water or drinks at:
- Camona da Terri CAS (Northern high hut)
- Capanna Scaletta SAT (Southern exit balcony hut)

## Renting equipment 🛍️
Because this is an alpine trekking pass crossing rather than a classic vertical cable park, specialized via ferrata sets and lanyards are not used. Robust high-grade mountaineering boots with stiff vibram outsoles and trekking poles are mandatory.

## Donation 💶
The chain handrails, stone markers, and suspension spans across the plain are maintained by the Swiss Alpine Club (SAC) and SAT Lucomagno. Staying or eating at the local huts directly funds backcountry alpine path security updates!

## My review ⭐️
An otherworldly and legendary high-alpine wonderland! Hiking across this expansive, treeless plateau with wide braided rivers meandering silently through raw meadows feels closer to Scandinavia or Iceland than Switzerland. The massive suspension bridge adds a fantastic rush of exposure right at the start, and the heavy chain links on the western descent provide fantastic security when navigating wet rock slabs down to Ticino. It is a long, strenuous day, but the absolute isolation is unmatched!
