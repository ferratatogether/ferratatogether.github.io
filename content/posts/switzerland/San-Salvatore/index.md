## Route overview ℹ️
A highly athletic, sport-style via ferrata built directly into the steep limestone of **Monte San Salvatore's northwest wall**. Dedicated to mountain guide Delio Ossola, this route demands serious upper-body strength and a solid head for heights due to its massive, dizzying exposure. 

The climb features up to 250 meters of thick steel cable running over highly direct vertical slabs with sparse iron steps, forcing you to utilize the natural rock friction. It kicks off instantly with a grueling, near-vertical wall (*D* rating), passes a tight mid-way grassy ledge where you can briefly shake out your forearms, and concludes with a sustained, slightly overhanging exit pillar (*C/D to D*) hanging directly above the lake.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! The smooth limestone faces become slick and exceptionally dangerous when damp; it must be avoided completely in wet or stormy weather.

## Travelling home 🏠

Funiculars descend from the summit station every 30 minutes straight back to Paradiso to link seamlessly with trains heading north via Lugano SBB.

Completely absent once inside the steep, vertical chimney shadows. Ensure you pack a full hydration bottle before clipping into the cables.

## My review ⭐️
A short but incredibly spicy bicep burner! It skips the scenic hiking and drops you straight onto an exposed, vertical wall that will test your arm strength and foot placement instantly. There are very few iron rungs compared to other Swiss routes, making it feel closer to true sport climbing. The view looking directly down between your feet at the deep blue of Lake Lugano and the city is spectacular! Tight climbing gloves are highly recommended.
