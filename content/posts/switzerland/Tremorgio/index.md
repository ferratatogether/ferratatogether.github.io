## Route overview ℹ️
Located on the dramatic Sasso Torrasco rock face between Mornera and Albagno, this via ferrata features exceptional exposure and views across the Magadino plains. It is divided into three distinct variants of 
increasing technical difficulty connected by an airy suspension bridge:

### Via Media (Blue Route)
Rated K3 (Intermediate). Follows accessible vertical rungs and steps, perfect for climbers getting comfortable with exposure.

### Via Difficile (Red Route)

Rated K4 (Difficult). Introduces steeper, prolonged vertical sections with fewer natural footholds.

### Via Super Difficile (Black Route)

Rated K5 (Very Difficult). An intense athletic challenge featuring a highly continuous, pumping overhanging crux that requires substantial upper body endurance.

## Season 🗓️

Principally accessible **year-round** due to its low elevation, though autumn, winter, and spring are highly preferred to avoid the punishing summer heat.

## My review ⭐️
An awesome sport climb with plenty of options! The black route section throws down a real challenge with crisp, persistent overhangs that test your forearm strength early. Crossing the exposed suspension walkway provides an excellent rush of pure exposure, and stopping off at Curzútt for a cold Merlot afterward is the perfect way to wrap up a Ticino climbing day!
