## Route overview ℹ️

A great, athletic limestone route in the Vaud Alps. Filled with vertical chimneys, tight cracks, and a challenging overhang that tests your foot placement.

The Via Ferrata de Planpraz in Leysin, Switzerland, is a challenging, highly physical K5 (Difficult/D) sport route known for demanding arm strength, overhanging sections, and playful features like rope bridges and vertical ladders

Graded K5 (very athletic), running mostly horizontally and vertically along a forested cliff band rather than scaling a high alpine peak

The climb is divided into three sections. If your arms get pumped early on the physical K5 overhangs, there is an intermediate escape route (échappatoire) before the final obstacles. Otherwise, you take the main exit at the top of the cliff after climbing the two final vertical ladders.

## By Car 🚗

Drive to Aigle and head up to Leysin village. The approach sits beneath the Leysin–Berneuse cable cars and is a 15-minute walk from Hôtel Le Grand Chalet 

{{< google-map-directions origin="Zurich" destination="Hôtel Le Grand Chalet" mode="d" height="500" >}}

## Parking 🅿️

You can drive up the narrow mountain road directly toward the route and use the small dirt parking area located just past the Parking - Leysin Lodge. From this spot, the approach trail to the base of the rock face is incredibly short

{{< osm lon="7.01643" lat="46.35023°" text="Parking" more="Leysin Lodge">}}

If you prefer wider, paved lots that are easier to access, you can park down in the village at the main Les Esserts Parking right by the Télécabin Berneuse lower station. From here, you will need to hike up past the back of the cable car station and follow the signs uphill through the forest to reach the start.

{{< osm lon="7.0135" lat="46.3458" text=Parking" more="Les Esserts" >}}

## Cable cars 🚠

Reached entirely on foot from the resort village, the Via Ferrata de Planpraz does not require taking a cable car to access its base

Once you top out at the main exit, the return path leads you down and follows the base of the Rochers de Planpraz rocks back to the start of the via ferrata route. Because the cliff band sits directly above the resort, the entire descent is a quick 20 to 35-minute walk down through the woods, leading right back to the parking lot by the ski lifts or the Leysin-Feydey train station
