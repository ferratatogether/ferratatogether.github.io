## Route overview ℹ️
The **Androsace (Red / Right Route)** is a short, explosive, and intensely aerial high-alpine sport route. Perched at an extreme elevation exceeding **3,000 metres**, it scales the rugged vertical rock pillars of the **Monts de Cion** ridge line. 

The climb forces you straight onto steep vertical faces with athletic rung spacing, short overhanging corners, and shaky rope bridges. Because it is designed for experienced climbers, you can utilize the beginner-friendly companion *Edelweiss* (Blue) route to warm up your muscles first before clipping directly into this K5 test piece. Both lines are equipped with the specialized **"Prisme Aéroligne" continuous lifeline system** to completely prevent accidental clip detachment.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! Given its severe high-altitude setting at 3,000m, a sudden drop in temperature, summer snow, or thunder risks can turn this metal cable installation highly dangerous instantly.

## Season 🗓️

Due to the extreme alpine setting and winter glacier operations, the route has a highly compressed seasonal window, operating daily from **mid-July through mid-September**.

Drive past Martigny along the Grand-Saint-Bernard route, turn cleanly at Sembrancher into the Val de Bagnes, and climb up the wide mountain curves past Châble directly into Verbier resort center.

## My review ⭐️
An elite high-alpine masterpiece with a breathtaking view of the Tortin glacier and Mont Fort! Climbing at 3,000 meters makes you feel like an authentic mountaineer. The exposure over the edge is outstanding, and the rope bridges require precise balance. Since it sits at such high altitude, it can get bitterly cold and windy very fast—make sure to pack an athletic windstopper and use robust climbing gloves!
