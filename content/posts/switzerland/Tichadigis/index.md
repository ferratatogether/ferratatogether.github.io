## Route overview ℹ️
The Via Ferrata du Tichodrome is an elegant horizontal traverse cutting right across the broad, vertical limestone barrier of **La Clusette**. Arranged in the western Swiss style, it focuses heavily on movement fluidity over raw strength, as it follows continuous systems of natural rock ledges. 

The route is **550 metres long** and handles exposure gracefully, featuring short tight sections where taller climbers must duck slightly beneath low rock overhangs and natural tunnels. It is highly welcoming for beginners, families, and young climbers looking to build confidence without punishing vertical overhangs.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! The smooth Jurassic limestone will lose all friction instantly when wet.

## Season 🗓️

Strictly open from **July 1 through December 31**. Note that it is **closed annually from January 1 to June 30** to protect the rare wallcreeper birds (*Tichodroma muraria*) nesting on the rock barrier faces. The first and last safety lines are completely decoupled on New Year's Eve to prevent off-season access.

## Cable cars 🚠
*No mountain cable cars exist for this route.* The entire cliff band is accessed strictly via standard forested approach paths.

## By Car 🚗

Drive past Neuchâtel on the H10 motorway heading into the Val de Travers, exit at Noiraigue, or climb toward Les Ponts-de-Martel via the cantonal Pass road.

## Parking 🅿️

Park explicitly at the large public car park adjacent to the **Restaurant Haut de la Côte (1035m)**. *Please note: Parking directly by the narrow forest entrance of the via ferrata itself is strictly forbidden!*

## My review ⭐️
The quintessential "panoramic traverse" route of western Switzerland! It completely avoids painful overhanging rungs and instead lets you comfortably tip-toe across incredible natural limestone highways. The exposure looking down at the Val de Travers loop is wonderful, and the trail setup makes it a stellar confidence-booster for beginners. Plus, it places you right next to the famous Creux du Van for a perfect weekend adventure double-feature!
