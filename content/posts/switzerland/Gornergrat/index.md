## Route overview ℹ️
An absolute masterpiece of dynamic canyoning and climbing! The [Gorner Gorge Adventure](https://www.zermatters.ch/en/activities/climb/via-ferrata/article/gorge-adventure/ "Gorge Adventure via ferrata") takes you through a breathtaking, water-carved river canyon beneath the gaze of the Matterhorn. The route is traversed **strictly descending** from the top down and is packed with technical challenges:
### The Descent
The adventure kicks off with a mandatory 10-metre vertical abseil into the damp canyon floor.
### The Challenges 
You will cross shaking Himalayan suspension bridges, traverse smooth glacier-polished rock walls, ride **three independent zip lines (Tyroliennes)**, and execute a thrilling giant pendulum swing over the roaring waters.

*Note: Individual solo climbing is strictly prohibited; you must book a slot with a certified mountain guide.*

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! However, because it sits deep inside a sheltered gorge, it is uniquely **operable all year round**. In winter, the frozen waterfalls and ice columns create a surreal ice-climbing backdrop.

Open **365 days a year** (conditions and guide availability permitting). The prime warm-water season runs from June through October.

## By Car 🚗

Zermatt is completely car-free. You must drive to the large multi-story terminal parking garage in **Täsch** and take the 12-minute shuttle train up to Zermatt station.

## Water 🚰
The river roaring beneath you is icy glacier runoff and not safe to drink untreated. Bring your own water flask.

Get water or drinks at:
- Bergrestaurant Blatten (located right at the gorge trail exit)
- Bakeries and supermarkets in Zermatt center

## My review ⭐️
An unparalleled adrenaline rush! This is completely different from a standard vertical face climb. Flying across roaring glacial waters on a zip line and throwing yourself into a massive pendulum swing deep inside an ancient rock canyon feels entirely surreal. It is an ideal confidence builder for active beginners, and because it runs completely dry, it's incredibly fun. Don't forget a solid pair of thin gloves!
