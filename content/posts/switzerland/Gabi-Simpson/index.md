## Route overview ℹ️
The Gabi Simplon via ferrata is a highly spectacular, heavily engineered sport route climbing a **250-metre high rock barrier** directly above the roaring mountain highway. The climb is broken into key sequential steps:

### The River Base
Kicks off right beside a gravel pit. You can cross an optional, shaky **three-rope monkey bridge** suspended directly over the rushing Doveria mountain stream.

### The Tunnel Gallery
Climbs straight up onto the outer face of the concrete road tunnel portal. You traverse a long horizontal ledge shelf (natural rock gallery) running right above the roaring traffic. Two independent intermediate emergency escape routes are located here.

### The Vertical Wall
The ultimate test section. Turns strictly vertical for the final 180 meters. It forces you up continuous ladders and iron brackets, passing a prominent Swiss flag fixed to the face before topping out onto a scenic viewing ridge.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! Because it features a southeast exposure, it is highly attractive as a clear shoulder-season trip, but it can get very hot or slick during severe storms.

## Cable cars 🚠
*No cable car networks are used.* The entry line is accessed directly on foot right next to the valley pass transit road.

## By Car 🚗

Drive from Brig straight up over the wide alpine Simplon Pass highway heading south toward Italy until you reach the small cluster of houses at Gabi.

## Parking 🅿️

Park explicitly at the designated **Gabi Ferrata / Bus turnaround parking lot (1210m)**. *Crucial note: Ensure you park defensively tightly to the edges so the large yellow Swiss Postbuses have ample room to turn safely!*

## Water 🚰
Completely dry once climbing above the river canyon bed. Carry sufficient fluids to handle the final vertical ladder pushes.

Get water or drinks at:
- Local traditional inns and bakeries in Simplon Dorf village center (3km up the road)

## Donation 💶
The route is open completely free of charge to the public. A voluntary donation box (*Kollekte*) is located near the trailheads to fund the regular winter stress testing and heavy anchor line updates managed by the local tourism boards.

## My review ⭐️
A fascinating, wildly industrial climbing experience! Stepping across the iron rungs while heavy trucks rumble directly below your boots feels completely surreal and delivers a unique, urban-alpine kind of exposure. The ladder segments on the upper vertical wall will definitely get your arms pumping, but the incredible abundance of iron pins makes it highly secure and predictable. It serves as a perfect, quick afternoon stop when driving over the Simplon Pass!
