## Route overview ℹ️
An alpine sport-climbing class route traversing the steep, vertical walls of Le Moléson (2002m). This via ferrata operates as a strict **one-way trail with no intermediate exits** and is split into two choices departing from the same cliff base:

### Le Pilier (Blue/Left Route)

Rated K4 (Difficult). Climbs up the sunnier northeast pillar, alternating between vertical steps and airy panoramas.

### La Face (Red/Right Route - Voie Hohl)

Rated K5 (Very Difficult). Scales the raw, shaded North Wall following an old athletic climbing route. It forces you straight into sustained vertical rungs, exposed grassy shelves, and a physical, highly overhanging athletic traverse near the end.

Both variants unite dynamically right below the fore-summit cross before leading up onto the main peak ridge.

## Water 🚰
Completely absent inside the sheer, rock chimney faces. Bring a full hydration container before entering the cable networks.

Get water or drinks at:
- Restaurant de Plan-Francey (Funicular mid-station)
- Summit Restaurant (Le Moléson mountain top terminal)

## Donation 💶
A nominal **CHF 4.- preservation fee per person** is collected at the ticket windows. This contribution funds safety line updates, replacing safety anchors, and structural trail maintenance on the cliffs.

## My review ⭐️
A spectacular Prealps wall climbing experience! If you want a real forearm burn, pick the right branch ("La Face"). Scaling up the exposed, shadowy face feels immensely rewarding, and the overhanging crux traverse right near the top provides plenty of pure exposure thrill. Make sure to buy a block of Gruyère cheese at the bottom dairy farm to celebrate after you finish!
