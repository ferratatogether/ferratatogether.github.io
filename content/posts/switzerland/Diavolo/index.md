## Route overview ℹ️

A truly spectacular route in the granite rocks of the Schöllenen gorge, albeit with plenty of grass in between. There are fascinating views down into the wild gorge and its roads. With 700 metres of wire rope, 265 iron pins and pitons, artificial footholds and two attached ladders. Not advisable when wet (grass). Moderately difficult via ferrata, ideal for beginners. 

{{< notice info>}} 👨‍⚖️
Newcomers [MUST sign the waiver form](https://forms.gle/phYLQZ5mvnNeuR9N9), it contains detailed information about the risks difficulty and more

{{< sac-scale level="T1" >}}
Andermatt (1436 m), station for the Furka-Oberalp-Bahn railway and terminus for the funicular coming up from Göschenen. Parking at the station. From the station at Andermatt, follow the route alongside and on the main road (route signed) through the Urner Loch into the Schöllenen gorge, turning right before you reach the Teufelsbrücke Bridge and making for the Suvorov Memorial (1,405 m).

From the Teufelsbrücke parking spaces, walk along the historical pedestrian paths heading north toward the old stone bridge portal. Cross safely through the pedestrian tunnel galleries to locate the starting cable placard situated right against the open granite rock wall base.{{< /sac-scale >}}

## My review ⭐️
Beginner friendly K2

- Very scenic
- Very very easy, lot of pig tail and iron steps
- Not vertical, more an inclined wall, you can walk like a dog :-)
- Rated at 2h, finished in 1.2h if experienced, take last part optional K3
- Easy access mostly flat 15min from main station andermatt
- Easy exit, 15min up then down 90min in the shadow of the forest
- Andermatt not so nice as a city
- Shop and WC in train station
