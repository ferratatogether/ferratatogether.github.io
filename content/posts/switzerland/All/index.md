Switzerland features over 80 via ferrata (protected climbing routes, or Klettersteige) spanning the Alps, Pre-Alps, and deep gorges

### Regional Overviews

### Valais
Home to high-alpine and extreme routes like Jägihorn, Daubenhorn, Evolène, and the dramatic Farinetta in Saillon.

### Bernese Oberland
Features family-friendly and adrenaline tracks like Mürren, Eiger-Rotstock, and Kandersteg-Allmenalp.

### Eastern Switzerland & Grisons
Features iconic limestone routes like Sulzfluh in St. Antönien and the historic Pinut.

### Ticino & Central Switzerland
Includes steep granite adventures like Diavolo in the Schöllenen Gorge and Fürenwand.
