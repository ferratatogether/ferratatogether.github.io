## Route overview ℹ️
Considered one of the most stunning yet demanding sport-style via ferratas in Switzerland, this route cuts directly into the vertical limestone cliffs of the Northwest face. **Strong arms are mandatory**, as there are intense, vertical-to-overhanging sections. The climb follows this distinct sequence:

### The Initial Spur

A massive 50-meter vertical wall (K5-) that gets your forearms pumping instantly.

### The Rope Bridge & Cave

A short rope bridge leads directly into a natural cave (*Gouffre de Niton*), giving climbers a shaded place to catch their breath. Note that due to long-term geological conservation, the deep inner cave paths are closed to hikers until late 2027/2028, but the outer climber resting area remains completely accessible.

### The Upper Splits

Following a diagonal ramp across exposed grassy ledges, you reach a definitive junction:

#### Left (The Escape Route / Échappatoire)

A 40-meter vertical corner crack rated K4. *Note: You must use this exit from May 1 to June 30 due to local bird nesting protections.*

#### Right (The Extreme Finish)

A highly exposed slab leading directly into a daunting, athletic overhanging roof crux (K5+).

## Season 🗓️

Open from **May through October**. The final extreme section is strictly closed from **May 1 to June 30** to protect rare breeding birds.

## By Car 🚗

Drive along the A9 motorway until you hit exit 15 (Montreux). To bypass the mountain train entirely, you can brave the narrow, winding mountain road climbing directly up past Caux to reach the Col de Jaman mountain pass restaurant.

## Water 🚰
Completely dry on the vertical limestone face. Ensure you pack plenty of water to counter muscle fatigue.

Get water or drinks at:
- Le Manoire Restaurant (Col de Jaman)
- Alpin Restaurant (Rochers de Naye Summit)

## My review ⭐️
This is an absolute masterpiece of exposure, but it demands serious respect. The first 50 meters throw you right into the void and will test your arm strength immediately. Breathtaking views over Lake Geneva keep you company the entire time. If you attempt the final K5+ roof section after June, make sure your arms aren't already cooked, as the overhang pulls hard!
