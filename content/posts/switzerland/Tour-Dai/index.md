## Route overview ℹ️
The Tour d'Aï via ferrata is a short, explosive, and highly panoramic sport route that scales the rugged west face of Leysin’s iconic peak. This is a strict **one-way trail with no intermediate exits**. 

Following the line known as the *"Voie des Vires"*, the route leads climbers along a continuous series of narrow, airy rock ledges running diagonally upwards. It rapidly turns vertical, forcing you through a memorable, tight vertical chimney and past a minor, pumping **overhanging crux wall** that requires arm strength. The exposed final tier sweeps over an open ridge line to finish right at the summit cross (2331m).

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! Because the upper section loops into the cooler, shaded side of the mountain massif, rock steps dry slowly and can remain slippery and cold.

From the top station of the Berneuse gondola, descend northwards on paths down into a grassy basin near Lac d'Aï (1905m). Continue straight ahead, climbing past the chairlift line up onto the grassy ridge. Traverse to the right along the scree fields beneath the cliffs to reach the starting cable placard along the west wall.{{< /sac-scale >}}

## Water 🚰
Completely absent once you step onto the steep cliff wall sections. Bring a proper hydration bottle.

Get water or drinks at:
- Le Kuklos Revolving Restaurant (La Berneuse peak station)
- Buvette des Chalets d'Aï (Alpine hut along the descent trail loop)

Access to the steel installation steps is completely free. The paths are maintained by the local alpine rescue and ski lift entities; supporting local mountain tourism keeps the anchors safely checked.

## My review ⭐️
An amazing, highly vertical wall experience with unmatched views! While it isn't overly long, the exposure is incredible from the very first steps. Moving across the narrow ledge paths gives you an awesome sensation of hanging directly above the valley floor, and turning around to see Lake Geneva gleaming below is jaw-dropping. Pack a light windbreaker layer, as the upper chimneys sit in deep mountain shade and get chilly quickly!
