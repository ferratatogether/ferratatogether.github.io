## Route overview ℹ️
Intermediate exits are available before the final tier for those who want to bypass the most demanding overhangs. 

The Via Ferrata d’Evolène (also known as Via Ferrata Rocs de Villa) is a spectacular, French-style sports via ferrata located in the Val d’Hérens within the Valais Alps. Characterised by its steep, compact limestone walls and intense, exposed overhangs, it offers jaw-dropping views of the valley and the glaciers of Dent Blanche and Pigne d’Arolla. It is open seasonally from early May to the end of October, depending on snow conditions.

The route is intelligently designed in three distinct stages, each featuring its own intermediate escape exit. This allows you to tailor the climb to your skill level or opt out before the most punishing sections:

### Part 1: Mur du Grand Duc (K4+ | Difficult)
Right from the starting line, you are thrown onto a vertical wall with vertical ladder sections and minor, arm-pumping overhangs. A long, airy, ascending traverse leads you to a highly exposed ladder. If your arms are already burning here, you should use the first exit.

### Part 2: Vire des Chamois (K3 | Moderate)
This section consists of a flat, horizontal ledge traverse. While it is highly exposed, it is technically straightforward and even accessible to older children or families who want to experience the heights without the physical strain (you can bypass Part 1 entirely and start directly here).

### Part 3: Le Grand Mur (K5+ | Extremely Demanding)
This final wall is reserved strictly for experienced, athletic climbers with excellent upper body strength. It features severe, sustained athletic overhangs that pull hard on your arms. Emerging from this section brings you to the "Sortie des Héros" (Heroes' Exit) paneled rest stop.

The rock face faces east, meaning it gets early morning sun but falls into the shadow by the afternoon. Climbing in the morning gives you great light, while an afternoon climb keeps you cool but can be chilly if the wind picks up

## Cable cars 🚠
*No cable cars are required for this route.* The path is entirely accessible on foot starting right from the edge of the village.

## By Car 🚗

Drive along the A9 motorway until you reach exit 27 (Sion-Est). Follow the Route d'Hérens up through Vex and past the historic Euseigne Pyramids directly into the Val d'Hérens.

## Water 🚰
No access on the rock face. Make sure to bring at least 1.5L of water to combat hydration loss during the strenuous overhangs.

Get water or drinks at:
- Coop Supermarket Evolène (located 200m before the trailhead parking lot)
- Local cafes in Evolène village

## My review ⭐️
An incredible upper-body test piece! The initial pitch wakes your muscles up instantly. The middle traverse lets you catch your breath and enjoy the valley views before you hit the final roof overhangs. The exposure on the final tier is wild—highly recommended as an afternoon climb to catch the sunset glowing over the Val d'Hérens.
