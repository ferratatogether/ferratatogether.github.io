## Route overview ℹ️
The via ferrata sections in the Albigna basin are integrated into the breathtaking **Gran giro del lago** alpine loop (T5). Unlike classic standalone sport climbing parks, this is a raw high-alpine trail where safety chains and iron rungs protect exposed granite bands on the wild west side of the lake. 

The total circular loop around the reservoir takes roughly 4 to 6 hours. The via ferrata elements are traversed moving counter-clockwise: crossing the massive dam wall, hiking to the **Capanna da l'Albigna CAS (2336m)**, navigating the rocky path on the east side across pathless granite blocks to the Albigna glacier mouth, and finally utilizing the safety chains and iron setups to ascend back out along the vertical rock ribs of the western lakeshore.

## Weather ☀️

Weather can cancel/abort/shorten the event a few days before if weather is not perfect for execution! Storms or damp mountain fog can make the high-altitude granite blocks and glacier crossings highly treacherous.

## Season 🗓️

Typically **mid-June through mid-October**. The trail is directly linked to the summer operational period of the local valley cable car system.

## By Car 🚗

Drive past Chur over the scenic Julier Pass down into the Engadin valley, pass through Maloja, and follow the winding mountain road down into the Val Bregaglia until you reach the Pranzaira cable car station loop between Casaccia and Vicosoprano.

## My review ⭐️
A breathtaking, pure wilderness loop! This is not a man-made adventure park—it is a rugged alpine exploration path. Climbing the rocky granite ribs with the massive glacier tongue directly over your shoulder and the blue lake glowing below is unforgettable. Crossing the narrow footbridges over roaring glacial streams provides an incredible rush. It requires sturdy boots and secure footwork, but the granite friction is second to none!
