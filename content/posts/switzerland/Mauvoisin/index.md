## Route overview ℹ️
This highly unique route runs deep inside the spectacular **Dranse Gorge**, directly under the massive Mauvoisin arch dam. It is split into **two distinct sections**:
1. **Saxifrage (Blue / Part 1):** Rated K3 (Quite difficult). It features a 250m long cable stretch and tight rung spacing built specifically so children from age 8 can safely climb. It crosses the river on a 15-meter rope bridge.
2. **Tichodrome (Red / Part 2):** Rated K4+ (Difficult). This part increases the exposure significantly with continuous athletic traverses, short vertical walls, and up to six independent rope bridges suspended up to 40 meters directly above the roaring glacial river.

## Season 🗓️

Open daily from **June 25 / July 1 through October 31**. Note that it is strictly closed in spring to protect rare wallcreeper birds (*Tichodroma muraria*) breeding on the rock faces.

## Cable cars 🚠
*No mountain railways are needed.* The route is entirely accessible on foot starting right from the edge of the access road.

## By Car 🚗

Take the A9 motorway until you reach Martigny, continue along cantonal road 21 to Sembrancher, and then drive down the Route de la Vallée straight through Le Châble and Fionnay up to the head of the valley.

## My review ⭐️
An atmospheric and chilly adventure! Climbing with the sound of roaring water beneath you is absolutely incredible. Part 1 is a relaxed warm-up, but Part 2 really switches things up with several shaky, prolonged three-cable bridges that challenge your balance. Because it sits deep in a shaded canyon, it remains very cold even in mid-summer—make sure to pack a reliable windstopper layer!
