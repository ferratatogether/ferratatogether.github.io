**Last Updated: October 17, 2026**

At **Ferrata Together** (https://github.io), we are committed to protecting the privacy of our community members. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you browse our website or participate in our community platforms, including Meetup and WhatsApp.

Because we are a non-profit, volunteer-run hobby group, we do not operate complex commercial tracking systems or sell user data. 

---

### 1. Information Gathered via Our Website
* **Passive Browsing:** Our website is a static site hosted via GitHub Pages. We do not use tracking cookies, require user accounts, or maintain local databases of personal details on this platform.
* **Server Logs:** GitHub may collect basic technical metrics (such as your IP address, browser type, and timestamp) for standard operational security and performance logging. We do not have control over or direct access to this data for marketing or profiling purposes.

### 2. Information Shared on Third-Party Communication Channels
Our community utilizes **Meetup.com** and **WhatsApp** to organize weekend events and maintain group communication. When you interact with us on these platforms, your information is handled as follows:
* **WhatsApp Community & Groups:** Joining our WhatsApp group makes your phone number, profile name, and profile picture visible to other community members according to your personal WhatsApp privacy settings. **Ferrata Together organizers do not store, harvest, or download your contact numbers for external use.** 
* **Meetup Profiles:** When signing up for events, any information you make public on your Meetup profile (name, photo, answers to RSVP questions) is accessible to the event hosts and other members of the group.

### 3. Media Policy (Photos & Videos)
* **Shared Event Albums:** Ferrata Together events are highly social activities. Participants frequently take photos and videos during climbs and share them within the community WhatsApp chat or on the platform.
* **Content Use:** By participating in our events, you acknowledge that your likeness may appear in these shared group photos. If you do not wish to appear in photos, please inform the organizers and your fellow climbers at the start of the event. 
* **Removal Requests:** If a photo containing your likeness is posted to our website or official community channels and you wish for it to be removed, contact an organizer directly, and we will delete it promptly.

### 4. Data Retention
We do not actively download or store your personal information offline. Your presence in our WhatsApp groups and Meetup groups persists only as long as you choose to remain a member. You can leave these groups at any point using the native exit tools provided by those platforms.

### 5. Third-Party Links
Our website contains links to external tools, maps, regional mountain guides, emergency services, and community platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of these external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

### 6. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our community tools or structural organization. Any modifications will be updated on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
