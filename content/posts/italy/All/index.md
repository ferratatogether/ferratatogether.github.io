Italy has over 400 via ferrata routes, primarily concentrated in the Dolomites and around Lake Garda, with regional maps and info accessible through community sites like Ferrate365 and local CAI (Club Alpino Italiano) sections.

## Top Regions and Notable Routes
### The Dolomites
Home to historic World War I paths and stunning limestone spires. Iconic routes include Via Ferrata Brigata Tridentina, Via Ferrata Bocchette Centrali in the Brenta group, and Via Ferrata Giovanni Lipella on Tofana di Rozes.

### Lake Garda & Trentino
Famous for dramatic cliffside traverses overlooking the water. Top routes include the historic Sentiero Contrabbandieri near Arco and the challenging Rino Pisetta.

### Other Regions
Routes extend outside the Alps as well, such as the Pan di Zucchero coastal ferrata in Sardinia and climbs in the Aosta Valley.
