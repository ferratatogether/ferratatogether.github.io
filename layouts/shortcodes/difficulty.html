Difficulty Level Austrian (Schall) German/Swiss (Hüsler) French / Alpine Terrain Description
Easy A K1 F (Facile) Flat to inclined walking terrain. Secured paths with ladders or cables used mostly for balance. Accessible to beginners.
Moderate B K2 PD (Peu Difficile) Steeper, more exposed rock terrain. Requires some arm strength. Handholds and footholds are readily available.
Difficult C K3 AD (Assez Difficile) Steep to vertical rock walls. Requires good physical fitness and upper body strength. Prolonged exposed sections.
Very Difficult D K4 D (Difficile) Vertical to overhanging sections. Often very exposed with sparse iron rungs. Requires high physical strength and experience.
Extremely Difficult E K5 TD (Très Difficile) Long, consistently overhanging sections. Demands extreme arm strength, flawless technique, and high mental resilience.
Ultra Difficult F K6 ED (Extrem. Difficile) Almost entirely overhanging, athletic, and highly physical. Reserved strictly for expert climbers with specialized training.
